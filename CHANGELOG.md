## Unreleased
- RERA withdrawals (0035, audit A13): a withdrawal from the designated account needs three certificates recorded first — architect (% of work done), engineer (% of work done) and CA (amount that may be withdrawn) — each with number, date and the file. Refused when a certificate is missing, of the wrong kind or project, dated after the withdrawal, or older than rera.cert_valid_days (90 days default, confirm with CA); when it goes beyond what the CA certified; or when total withdrawn would pass deposits x certified work done (the lower of architect and engineer). The checks are inside the insert, so two withdrawals at once cannot both slip through. The architect's certificate sets the project's certified completion. Certificate entries are never deleted: the Owner can cancel one that no withdrawal relies on. Accounts can now upload files from the RERA screen.
- Controls (0034, audit priorities 1 and 2). Hard stops: no booking, cost sheet or demand before the MahaRERA number is entered and the registration approval is approved; no possession before the OC (part OC per wing allowed), and possession only by the PM/Owner (dues need the Owner's written reason, open snags or checklist items a written reason). Approvals: allowed moves only (going back from approved needs the Owner and a reason), "Approved" needs the letter number and date (optional file), a CC records "work allowed up to floor N", approvals can depend on others (the Mumbai checklist links IOD → CC → plinth CC → further CC, CC → MahaRERA, further CC + fire NOC → OC). Milestones: the site engineer marks "reached", the PM certifies with the architect's certificate; only certification releases buyer demands; no future dates; a payment milestone must name its wing when the project has more than one; work above the CC floor is stopped (the Owner may go past it with a written reason); certification can be undone with a reason (unsent demands go back to waiting, sent unpaid ones go on hold). Contractors: variations (extra quantity or new items; above Rs 5 lakh the Owner approves), joint measurements (site engineer + contractor's person; approved by someone else), RA bills refused beyond the work order + approved variations or beyond approved measurements (the old "save anyway" is gone), the checker cannot also approve, the approval limit is on gross work done, advance recovered automatically at the order's rate (10% default; less needs a reason), a work order cannot close with advance outstanding, retention is held 60 months from the OC (early release: Owner with a reason). Purchase: vendor bills checked against the PO's vendor, duplicates refused, bills beyond material received x PO rate (+3%) go on hold until the PM releases them with a note; material received beyond the PO (+3%) needs the PM/Owner with a reason; the note-only receipt is refused for POs with items. Fixes: RA bill saved in one step (no bill without lines), IST dates for milestones and task lateness, milestone cannot be certified twice, daily reports cannot be future-dated (older than 2 days: PM only), transit rent scheduled beyond 48 months (up to 10 years), TDS rate without PAN is a setting, decimals (0.3) accepted in every number box. New settings: con.variation_owner_limit_paise (Rs 5 lakh), pro.tolerance_bp (3%), con.dlp_months (60), con.advance_recovery_bp (10%), tax.tds_no_pan_bp (20%).
