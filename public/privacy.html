Privacy Policy

NirmaanCRM at crm.mysociety.tech · Last updated 21 November 2026

NirmaanCRM is a private business tool used by one real-estate development company and its staff to manage projects, approvals, drawings, site reports, purchasing and sales. It is not offered to the general public.

What we collect

Only what the company enters or uploads: names, email addresses and phone numbers of staff, customers and vendors; project and sales records; and documents such as drawings, approvals and agreements. Staff passwords are stored only as salted hashes.

Staff location

When a salesperson taps “Check in here” during a site visit, the phone’s location at that moment is saved with the visit, to confirm the visit took place at the project. It is saved only when the person taps the button, only after the phone asks their permission, and is never tracked in the background.

AI assistant

If the company switches on the AI assistant, the text of a question or a draft request, together with the relevant business records the person is allowed to see (never phone numbers, email addresses, PAN, Aadhaar or bank details), is processed by Cloudflare Workers AI to produce an answer. Questions and answers are not stored by the CRM, and the AI cannot change any record.

Google Drive access

If the company's Owner connects Google Drive, NirmaanCRM asks for one permission: drive.file. This lets the app see and manage only the files and folders it creates itself. It cannot read, list or change any other file in the connected Google account. The sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on the company's own Cloudflare account and are never shown in the app or shared with anyone.

NirmaanCRM's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

How data is used

Data is used only to run the company's own CRM: showing records to authorised staff, sending the company's own emails (for example a morning report or a login code) and letting the company share a document with a customer through an expiring link. We do not sell data, show advertising, or use data to train AI or machine-learning models. We do not share data with third parties except the infrastructure providers needed to run the service (Cloudflare for hosting and storage, Google for Drive files if connected, and the company's chosen email provider).

Who can see it

Access is limited by role (Owner, project manager, site engineer, sales, accounts, purchase, HR, viewer). Site engineers see only the projects they are assigned to. Customer share links expire, can be limited in number of views, and can be cancelled at any time.

Retention and deletion

Records stay until the company deletes them. The Owner can disconnect Google Drive at any time from Setup → Storage, and can revoke the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files created in Drive remain in that Drive until the Owner deletes them. To ask for data about you to be corrected or removed, contact the company that gave you access to NirmaanCRM.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Secrets are encrypted at rest, file downloads require sign-in, and actions are recorded in an audit log.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above is updated.
