NirmaanCRM — one-page cheat sheets

Print one page per person. Address: crm.mysociety.tech · Add to Home Screen on your phone for an app icon.

Owner

Every morning
  • Home: tap any tile (money in, overdue, bills due, POs waiting, site reports, snags, new leads).
  • Approve purchase orders: Procurement → Approve (you are the only approver above the PM limit).
  • Check Approvals for anything expiring within 30 days.
Weekly
  • Audit log and Shared links (cancel old customer links).
  • Users & access: deactivate anyone who left.
  • Download “Everything (backup)” from More. A nightly copy also goes to Drive/Backups.
Set up once
  • Branding & letterhead, Storage (Google Drive), Email, Users, Projects, payment plan, milestone keys.
Remember
  • You cannot deactivate or demote yourself. Keep your password private. Confirm approval dates with your CA/architect.

Project manager

  • Approvals: load the Mumbai checklist, mark Applied / Query / Approved with dates, set valid-till.
  • Drawings: “Issue new revision” → Issued For Construction. The old one becomes SUPERSEDED automatically and site is told.
  • Site: add milestones with a key (e.g. slab5); when site taps Done, matching customer payments become Ready.
  • Procurement: approve POs up to your limit; above it the Owner approves. You cannot approve a PO you created.
  • Snags: verify fixes after the engineer marks them fixed.

Site engineer

  • Daily (2 minutes): Site → Today’s work report → what happened, labour, photos → Save. No signal? It saves on the phone and sends itself later. The orange box shows what is waiting.
  • Drawings: you only see the latest “Issued For Construction” drawing. If it says SUPERSEDED, do not build from it.
  • Snag: Site → Raise a snag, add photos. After fixing, tap Mark fixed.
  • Material arrived: Procurement → Material received on the PO.
  • Language: More → Language → मराठी / हिन्दी.
You cannot see
  • Sales, money, other projects.

Sales

  • New lead: Sales → New lead. Ask for consent. A duplicate mobile number is refused.
  • Book a flat: Flats → Book this flat. A flat cannot be booked twice.
  • Send a brochure: Brochures → Send to customer → WhatsApp. The link expires; the Owner can cancel it.
  • Receipt: open the booking → Record receipt (add TDS if deducted) → Print.
You cannot see
  • Internal price lists, purchasing, people records.

Accounts / Purchase

  • Purchase: Vendors → RFQ → add quotes (lowest and fastest are marked) → choose → New purchase order.
  • Accounts: Procurement → Add bill (due date), Mark paid. Vendor ledger: tap a vendor.
  • Reports: More → Download for Excel (receipts, receivables, purchases).
  • Print: PO, demand letter and receipt print on the company letterhead. DRAFT means not yet issued.

New in 2026: all roles

Everyone
  • Menu: the left menu shows only the groups for your job. Tap a heading to open it; several can stay open.
  • Offline: no signal? Keep working. The orange bar shows what is waiting and sends it by itself when the signal returns.
  • Install the app: tap the install icon (top bar) for iPhone, Android, Windows or Mac.
  • My profile: picture (you can crop it), mobile number, position. Team: say thank you to a colleague. My work report: charts, print or Excel.
  • Only the Owner can delete anything. Money is never deleted: it is reversed with a reason.
Site
  • Drawings: open the drawing and tap I have seen R3.
Accounts
  • Bank statement: upload the CSV, confirm the suggested matches, ignore the rest.
  • Demand letters: tick the ready demands, send by email, or print a sheet.
  • Send statement (on a booking): save the statement as PDF, choose it, share the link. Turn it off any time.
  • Tally export: type your ledger names once, check, download the XML. Not yet checked with your Tally: test one day first.
  • Redevelopment → Transit rent: set rent, record payments (cheque number), reverse with a reason.
Owner / PM
  • Redevelopment: society consent meter, members, rehab flats, corpus, meetings. Confirm the required majority with your advocate.
  • Feasibility: add a new version at regular intervals; old versions stay.
  • Website form: copy the link or embed code for enquiries.
  • Home shows Needs your attention; every line opens its list.

HR

  • People → Staff, Open positions, Interviews.
  • Open a position → add candidates → move them New → Screened → Interview (pick the time) → Offered → Hired. Hired people join Staff automatically.
Never store
  • Full Aadhaar numbers.
